- The review swarm no longer deadlocks when a PR's review runs out of redispatches ([#14361](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14361)). Under one review kick in flight per agent (#13794), every pending entry made its agent busy, including the entries an exhausted head deliberately keeps (so it is never re-kicked) and entries already past `pending_ttl` on a PR not yet reached this cycle. Three exhausted PRs pinned every review head and the decision agent, and the swarm dispatched nothing for any PR for about 36 hours while every reviewer sat idle. Only live entries (inside `pending_ttl`, on a head that is not exhausted) now count as load. Pending entries naming agents the hive no longer runs (a replica or head removed by lowering `replicas`) are pruned so their perspective is asked again, and an exhausted head is labelled with the `requires_human` verdict label instead of staying on `reviewing` indefinitely.
